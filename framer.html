What this means: Paying for editor seats on client sites? That cost just halved. But if you lean on Framer's AI agents heavily, watch your credit usage — it's no longer unlimited. The $100/mo Scale plan was also retired; Basic and Pro are now the only paid site plans.

On June 16, 2026, Framer cut its editor seat price in half and introduced a new $10/month Content Editor role alongside it. The same release retired the $100/month Scale plan, leaving Basic and Pro as the only paid site plans, and put AI agent usage behind a credit meter for the first time — Free gets 500 credits/day, Basic 1,000/month, Pro 3,000/month, with extra credits purchasable. A typical landing page costs about 300 credits to generate. Framer frames the seat cut as offsetting the new AI metering rather than as an unrelated discount. Framer's own announcement carries no dateline; two independent pricing-change trackers corroborate the June 16, 2026 date.
