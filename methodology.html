How we verify a change before logging it

Every entry on QuietHike goes through the same four steps before it's published. No step is skipped for a vendor we like, dislike, or have never heard of.

  1. Catch it

    A price change surfaces from a vendor's own changelog, a community forum thread, a support article update, or a discrepancy noticed while researching elsewhere.

  2. Verify against the vendor directly

    Before anything gets logged, we check the vendor's own current pricing page — never a third-party blog alone. If the vendor's own pages disagree with each other, we say so rather than picking one.

  3. Cross-check the "before" price

    The old price gets the same scrutiny as the new one — sourced from an archived page, a prior independent pricing tracker, or the vendor's own changelog, never guessed.

  4. Log it plainly

    What changed, what it actually means for a real buyer, and where to verify it yourself. No affiliate framing, no "which plan should you pick" — just what happened.

Real example from the log

quiethike/verify QuickBooks Online
01 → 02: found on QuickBooks' own pricing update page, not a reseller blog
- ~$99–115/mo (Plus, before Aug 1, 2026) [3P — disputed]
+ $140/mo (Plus, current, confirmed Aug 1, 2026) [VENDOR]
03 → 04: renewal-date language checked against a live 90%-off promo, kept separate from the standard rate
05: published with the historical figure labeled disputed, not silently resolved

What the source tags mean

[VENDOR] means the source is the vendor's own page. [3P] means a third-party source — a forum, a changelog mirror, an independent tracker. [3P — disputed] marks a figure we could source but couldn't confirm directly against the vendor — published as-is, but flagged rather than silently treated as fact. [UNABLE TO VERIFY] means we found a claim but couldn't confirm it anywhere reliable, so it's noted rather than asserted.

If we get something wrong

We don't quietly edit it. A correction stays attached to the original entry with what changed and why, and it's also listed on the Corrections page so nothing gets memory-holed.
