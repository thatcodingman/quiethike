Privacy Policy

What data QuietHike collects, and what it doesn't.

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

QuietHike is a static, read-only content site. We do not require account creation, and we do not collect names, emails, or personal information through any form on this site unless you explicitly contact us directly.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand traffic patterns — pages visited, general geographic region, device and browser type, and referring source. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors. Google's own privacy practices for this data are described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

This site may use minimal, functional cookies required for basic site operation and analytics. We do not use cookies for cross-site ad tracking or sell any cookie data to third parties.

Third-party links

QuietHike links out to vendor pricing pages and third-party sources as part of our sourcing methodology. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites you visit through these links — review their own privacy policies separately.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share visitor data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@quiethike.com.

QuietHike logs publicly verifiable pricing changes. We are not affiliated with, and do not receive payment from, any vendor mentioned. Confirm current pricing directly with each vendor before purchasing.
