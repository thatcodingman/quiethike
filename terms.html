Terms of Service

The rules for using QuietHike, in plain language.

Last updated: August 2026

What QuietHike is

QuietHike is a website that logs publicly verifiable pricing changes made by software vendors. We aggregate information from vendors' own pricing pages, help centers, and changelogs, along with independent third-party sources, and publish it as a running record.

Accuracy and no warranty

We verify every entry against the best sources available at the time of publication, and we label clearly when a figure comes from the vendor directly versus a third-party source. Pricing changes constantly and vendors update their pages without notice, so figures on this site may become outdated after publication. QuietHike is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. Always confirm current pricing directly with the vendor before making a purchasing decision.

Not affiliated with any vendor

QuietHike is independently operated. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or compensated by any company mentioned on this site. Company and product names are used for identification purposes only.

Acceptable use

You may read, share, and link to content on this site freely. You may not scrape or republish substantial portions of this site's content for a competing product without permission, attempt to disrupt or overload the site, or use the site in a way that violates applicable law.

Content ownership

Original written analysis, commentary, and the specific presentation of information on this site are owned by QuietHike. Underlying facts about vendor pricing are not owned by anyone and can't be — we're just the ones who verified and wrote them down.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the site evolves. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated terms.
