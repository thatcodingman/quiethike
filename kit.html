Kit retired its old $15 entry tier — and Creator now starts at $33/mo billed annually

- $15/mo (Creator, ~300 subscribers, legacy pricing)
+ $33/mo annual, $39/mo monthly (Creator, 1,000 subscribers)

What this means: If you signed up for Kit before September 2025, check your actual invoice — the old $15/month tier no longer exists for new signups and may not be honored at your next renewal either.

Share on X

This wasn't a gradual increase — the old low-tier pricing structure was retired outright in the September 2025 repricing. Anyone quoting Kit's "starts at $15" is quoting a price that no longer exists for new signups. Separately: as of September 2026, Kit's higher tier is mid-rebrand — its current marketing page is titled "Kit Pro," while the linked help-center article and the page's own URL slug still say "Creator Pro." At 1,000 subscribers, Pro currently starts at $79/month, or $66/month equivalent billed annually.
