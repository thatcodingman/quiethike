HubSpot moves Breeze Customer Agent and Prospecting Agent to outcome-based pricing

- Customer Agent: $1.00 per conversation, flat [VENDOR] — Prospecting Agent: flat monthly charge per enrolled contact, no public figure [VENDOR]
+ Customer Agent: $0.50 per resolved conversation, 50 Credits [VENDOR] — Prospecting Agent: $1.00 per qualified lead, 100 Credits [VENDOR]

What this means: You now only pay when the AI agent actually finishes the job — a resolved conversation or a qualified lead — not just for usage. That can mean lower bills for low-volume accounts, but it also ties cost directly to how well the agent performs. Both agents remain Pro/Enterprise-only, now with a free 28-day trial.

Share on X

On April 14, 2026, HubSpot restructured billing for its Breeze Customer Agent and Breeze Prospecting Agent around outcomes rather than raw usage. The Customer Agent now charges $0.50 (50 Credits) per resolved conversation instead of a flat $1.00 per conversation regardless of outcome. The Prospecting Agent now charges $1.00 (100 Credits) per qualified lead, replacing an earlier flat per-contact monthly charge that HubSpot never published a specific figure for. Both agents stay limited to Pro and Enterprise tiers of their respective hubs, and HubSpot added a free 28-day trial for each. HubSpot's credit system (1 Credit = $0.01) underlies the billing; credits are drawn from the account's existing HubSpot credit pool.
