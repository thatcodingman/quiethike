Figma price changes

Figma replaced per-product billing with unified Full, Dev and Collab seats in March 2025, and removed monthly billing from Organization and Enterprise.

Mar 11, 2025 Structural change Verified Rechecked Sep 30, 2026

Figma's per-product pricing became per-seat — and monthly billing disappeared from Organization and Enterprise

- Figma Design, Dev Mode, FigJam billed separately
+ Unified seat types (Full/Dev/Collab), $3–$90/seat/mo depending on plan and seat type

What this means: If your team is on an Organization or Enterprise plan, remember monthly billing is gone — budget annually so renewal doesn't catch you off guard.

Share on X

The bigger story isn't the sticker price — it's that Organization and Enterprise no longer offer monthly billing at all going forward, only annual. Teams with fluctuating headcount lost a flexibility option that existed before the overhaul, and it's easy to miss unless you're pricing out a renewal. Per-seat pricing (as of Sep 2026) breaks down as: Professional — Full $16/mo, Dev $12/mo, Collab $3/mo; Organization — Full $55/mo, Dev $25/mo, Collab $5/mo; Enterprise — Full $90/mo, Dev $35/mo, Collab $5/mo. Figma Design itself can't be purchased separately — it's bundled into the Full seat alongside Dev Mode, FigJam, and Figma Slides.
